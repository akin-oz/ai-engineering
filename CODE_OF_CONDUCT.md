# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making participation in AI Engineering OS welcoming and
respectful. Contributors should communicate constructively, assume good
intent, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, and any conduct that would make a reasonable person
feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to [akin@akinzoztorun.com](mailto:akin@akinzoztorun.com).
Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. The
maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to project spaces and public spaces where an individual is
representing the project.
